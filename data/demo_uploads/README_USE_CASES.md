# Aiwana Demo Upload Pack

Upload these files from the Next.js UI at `http://localhost:3000`.

## Documents

- `01_aiwana_legal_contract_multimodal.pdf`
  - Tests contract clauses, parties, obligations, dates, penalties, legal risk, tables, and charts.
- `02_aiwana_research_report_multimodal.pdf`
  - Tests research summaries, table evidence, chart explanation, assumptions, risks, and recommendations.
- `03_aiwana_enterprise_policy_multimodal.pdf`
  - Tests controls, audit evidence, governance workflows, policy traceability, and process diagrams.
- `04_aiwana_scanned_ocr_multimodal.pdf`
  - Tests image-only scanned pages, OCR, missing evidence behavior, image panels, dates, penalties, and responsible teams.

## Suggested Questions

1. Which clause creates the highest termination notice risk, and what page supports it?
2. Who are the parties in the agreement, and what obligations have deadlines?
3. Explain the daily water availability chart in simple language.
4. Give a research-grade answer with Executive summary, Evidence, Risks, Assumptions, and Final recommendation.
5. Which enterprise controls require citations and what audit evidence proves them?
6. Trace the policy control risk and compliance evidence.
7. What does the scanned notice say about OCR evidence and the replacement due date?
8. Hinglish mein batao: graph kya show karta hai, citations ke saath?

## What To Watch

- The chat answer should include grounded citations.
- The Evidence Canvas should show page previews, citation snippets, confidence, and agent trace.
- The scanned OCR document may be slower because it is image-only.
- If an answer is unsupported, the verifier should abstain instead of inventing.
